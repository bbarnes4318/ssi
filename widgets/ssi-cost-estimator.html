About these figures

These figures are an estimate, not a quote and not an offer of insurance. They are based on market-average monthly premiums for simplified-issue final expense whole life coverage from MoneyGeek's 2026 rate analysis of final expense quotes across major carriers, updated August 12, 2026, using a baseline profile of a nonsmoker in average health. Your actual premium depends on your age, gender, tobacco use, health history, coverage amount, the carrier you select and your state of residence. Coverage availability and maximum face amounts vary by carrier and by age. Senior Solutions Insurance is a licensed insurance agency. Not all products are available in all states.

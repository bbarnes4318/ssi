    Final Expense Insurance FAQs

    What is Final Expense Insurance?

    Final expense insurance is a small whole life policy, usually $5,000 to $25,000, built to cover a funeral, burial or cremation, final medical bills and small outstanding debts. There is no medical exam, the premium is locked in for life on the day you are approved, and the benefit is paid in cash directly to the person you name.

    How much coverage can I get?

    Most carriers issue between $5,000 and $25,000, depending on your needs and budget. The rate table on this page shows $10,000, $20,000 and $30,000; $25,000 falls between the second and third columns.

    How much coverage do I need for final expenses?

    Start with the median funeral and burial cost of about $8,300, then add any outstanding medical bills, small debts and a margin for your family's immediate expenses. Most people land between $10,000 and $20,000. A licensed agent will work through the number with you on the call.

    Can I qualify if I have a pre-existing condition?

    Most applicants do qualify. Simplified issue asks yes/no health questions and requires no medical exam, and common conditions like controlled diabetes or high blood pressure usually pass. If your health history rules that out, guaranteed acceptance plans ask no health questions at all — they cost more and the full benefit begins after two years. A licensed agent tells you which one applies to you on the call — see which conditions affect approval.

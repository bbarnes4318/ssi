The price jumps hardest between 75 and 80

Average premiums rise 44% for women and 45% for men between ages 75 and 80 — the sharpest step in the whole table. If you are in your early seventies, the cheapest policy you will ever be offered is the one you buy this year.

Coverage caps tighten with age

Transamerica, one of the carriers we represent, allows up to $50,000 through age 55, $40,000 through 65, $30,000 through 75 and $25,000 through 85. Other carriers set their own limits. A licensed agent will tell you what is available at your age before you apply.

Two ways to qualify — and why one costs more

Final expense policies come in two kinds. Which one you are offered depends on your health, and it matters more than which carrier you pick.

Simplified issue — answer a few health questions

No medical exam. A short list of yes/no health questions on the application. Most people qualify, and coverage starts on day one. This is what the rate tables above show.

Guaranteed issue — no health questions at all

Guaranteed acceptance, regardless of health. It costs more — typically 40 to 80% more for the same age and coverage — and carries a two-year waiting period: if you pass away in the first two years for a reason other than an accident, most policies return the premiums you paid rather than the full benefit. After two years, the full benefit applies.

If you assume poor health rules you out, call anyway. A licensed agent can tell you in one phone call which of these two you qualify for, and with which carrier. Many people who expect guaranteed issue turn out to qualify for simplified issue and the lower rate.

Final expense insurance FAQs

What is Final Expense Insurance?

Final Expense Insurance is a life insurance policy designed to cover end-of-life costs like funeral expenses, medical bills, or unpaid debts.

How much coverage can I get?

Policies typically range from $5,000 to $25,000, depending on your needs and budget. The rate table on this page shows $10,000, $20,000 and $30,000; $25,000 falls between the second and third columns.

How much coverage do I need for final expenses?

This depends on your personal and family needs, but our agents can help you determine the right amount.

Can I qualify if I have a pre-existing condition?

Yes, most applicants can qualify for final expense insurance regardless of pre-existing conditions.

Final Expense Insurance FAQs

What is Final Expense Insurance?

Final Expense Insurance is a life insurance policy designed to cover end-of-life costs like funeral expenses, medical bills, or unpaid debts.

How much coverage can I get?

Policies typically range from $5,000 to $25,000, depending on your needs and budget. The rate table on this page shows $10,000, $20,000 and $30,000; $25,000 falls between the second and third columns.

How much coverage do I need for final expenses?

This depends on your personal and family needs, but our agents can help you determine the right amount.

Can I qualify if I have a pre-existing condition?

Yes, most applicants can qualify for final expense insurance regardless of pre-existing conditions.

Where It All Started

We continue to operate with our core values driving all of our partnerships. We've gone from representing one insurance company, where we helped thousands of families, to representing most of the nation's leading insurance carriers.

The founders of Senior Solutions Insurance have varied backgrounds in insurance, business, management, administration and finance. Everyone who works with us starts as an agent and learns every aspect of every product we offer before moving up.

— Chris Martin, Founder & CEO

Meet the Founder

Chris Martin is the founder and CEO of Senior Solutions Insurance. With 18 years as an insurance agent, he has personally helped over 5,000 families, and the agency has helped over 25,000 families in total. He started in 2008 selling door-to-door and built Senior Solutions into a nationally recognized agency representing major carriers.

He lives with his wife Sarah and their four children near the Rocky Mountains, where the family is active in church and community and enjoys foster parenting.

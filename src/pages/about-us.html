Our story

From one carrier, door to door, to most of the nation's leading carriers

Senior Solutions started in 2008 with one insurance company and Chris Martin knocking on doors. The idea has not changed since: people over 50 do not want to be sold to. They want a straight answer about what coverage costs and what they qualify for, from someone who will still be reachable next year.

Today the agency represents most of the nation's leading final expense carriers, along with Medicare Advantage and ACA marketplace plans. The team's backgrounds run through insurance, business, management, administration and finance, but everyone who works here starts the same way: as an agent, learning every product we offer before they move up.

What that means for you

The person who answers your call can compare carriers for your situation on the spot, and tell you plainly if a cheaper option exists.

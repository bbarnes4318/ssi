Our story

It started with one agent and a lot of door knocking

Chris Martin started Senior Solutions Insurance in 2008, selling final expense insurance door to door.

There was no large marketing operation behind him. He had to earn each conversation, learn the products and understand what families actually needed.

That experience shaped the agency that exists today.

Senior Solutions has grown into an established insurance agency serving families with final expense insurance, Medicare and ACA health coverage. The company remains based in Greenwood Village, Colorado.

How we work

Why our agents start with the products

Insurance is easier to sell than it is to understand.

Our agents learn the products they represent so they can explain the differences between carriers, underwriting approaches and coverage options in language a customer can actually use.

The goal is not to make insurance sound complicated. The goal is to make the decision easier.

Everyone who works here starts the same way: as an agent, learning every product we offer before they move up. A licensed agent can explain the carriers and coverage options available for your situation.

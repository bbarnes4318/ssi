Medicare Supplement

Original Medicare leaves some costs behind. Medigap can help with those costs.

Medicare Supplement Insurance, or Medigap, is private insurance that works alongside Original Medicare. It can help pay certain deductibles, coinsurance and other out-of-pocket costs that Original Medicare does not pay.

Medigap is not a Medicare Advantage plan. You generally need Original Medicare Part A and Part B to buy it, and Medigap does not pay Medicare Advantage costs.

Final expense insurance

A simple life insurance policy designed for the expenses your family will face.

Final expense insurance is usually a smaller whole life policy intended to leave money behind for a funeral, burial or cremation, final medical bills, small debts and other immediate expenses. The death benefit goes to the person you name, who can use the money where it is needed most.

What the money can pay for

Funeral or cremation costs, burial expenses, final medical bills, small debts or whatever else your family needs after your death.

How approval works

Many final expense policies require no medical exam. Some ask health questions; guaranteed-issue policies do not. Your health history can affect the price and the policies available to you.

How much coverage you need

Start with the expenses you want covered, then consider savings and other resources your family could use. Many people choose $10,000 to $20,000, but there is no one right amount.

Questions

The questions people ask before they buy.

Short answers here. The final expense insurance cost page goes deeper, and a licensed agent will answer anything else at 1-888-957-3337.

What is final expense insurance?

A smaller whole life policy, from $5,000 up to $50,000 depending on the carrier, designed to help pay for a funeral, burial or cremation, final medical bills and small debts. The benefit is paid to the person you name, and they can use it for whatever is needed.

How much does final expense insurance cost?

It depends on your age, gender, health, tobacco use and the amount of coverage. Using 2026 market averages, $10,000 of coverage runs about $30 a month for a 50-year-old woman and $38 for a man; at 80 the same coverage is about $125 and $164. Compare final expense insurance rates by age.

How much coverage do I need?

Start with what your family would actually have to pay: the funeral or cremation, burial costs, final medical bills and any small debts. Many families land between $10,000 and $20,000. See what a funeral costs before you settle on a number.

Do I need a medical exam?

No. Final expense policies do not require an exam, blood test or nurse visit. Simplified-issue policies ask a short list of yes-or-no health questions; guaranteed-issue policies ask none.

Can I qualify with health problems?

Often, yes. Carriers evaluate conditions differently, and common ones such as controlled high blood pressure or type 2 diabetes managed with pills are routinely accepted. For health histories that fail every carrier’s questions, guaranteed-issue coverage is available at a higher premium. See how specific conditions affect approval.

What is the difference between simplified issue and guaranteed issue?

Simplified issue asks health questions and requires no medical exam; if you qualify, it pays the full benefit from day one and often costs less. Guaranteed issue asks no health questions and requires no exam either, but it costs more and a graded benefit may apply during the first years of the policy. Compare the two in detail.

How soon can coverage begin?

On a simplified-issue policy the full benefit is generally in force from the day the policy is issued. On a guaranteed-issue policy the full benefit typically begins after a two-year graded period. Your agent confirms the effective date and any waiting period before you apply.

Does the premium increase as I get older?

On the whole life policies we sell, the premium is set by your age when the policy is issued and does not rise as you get older. That is one reason buying earlier costs less over the life of the policy.

Important consumer alert

Unauthorized calls using our company name

We have recently become aware that an unknown company or group is making unsolicited telephone calls to consumers and falsely identifying themselves as our company, or providing our company’s name and address.

These calls are not being made by us, and we have not authorized any other company to make these calls or use our identity. We take this matter very seriously and are actively working to identify the person or company responsible. We are asking for your help.

Please do not give the caller personal, financial, banking, Social Security or other sensitive information.

If you received one of these calls

If someone called you unsolicited and claimed to represent our company, help us find out who is making the calls by sharing as much information as you can, including:

  • The phone number that appeared on your caller ID
  • The date and approximate time of the call
  • The name of the person or company the caller claimed to represent
  • Any callback number or other contact information they provided
  • What the caller was offering, selling or discussing
  • The agent’s insurance license number (NPN)
  • The insurance company they said they represent
  • Any website, email address, company name or other identifying information mentioned during the call
  • A screenshot of the call information from your phone, if available
  • Any voicemail, text message or other communication you received in connection with the call

Help us identify who is responsible

If you have information about one of these calls, please contact us and mention “Unauthorized Call Report.”

Call1-888-957-3337 Emailadmin@ssi-medicare.com

We appreciate your help. Information from consumers may help us identify the company or individuals responsible and take appropriate action to stop the unauthorized use of our company’s name.

Please note

A caller knowing our company name or address does not mean the caller is affiliated with or authorized by our company. Company names and addresses are generally available through public sources and can be misused by third parties.

If you are unsure whether a call actually came from us, do not rely on the phone number the caller gave you. Contact us directly using the contact information published on this website.

Health insurance FAQs

What is the ACA Open Enrollment period?

Open Enrollment is the annual window when anyone can sign up for a marketplace plan or change the one they have. It generally runs from November into January, and exact dates vary by state. Outside that window you need a qualifying life event — losing job coverage, moving, marriage, or a new child — to enroll.

Are subsidies available to lower my premium?

Often, yes. Premium tax credits are based on your household income and household size, and they are applied directly to your monthly premium rather than paid back at tax time. We check what you qualify for before you pick a plan, so you are comparing what you would actually pay.

What is ACA Health Insurance?

ACA plans are the individual and family health plans sold through the health insurance marketplace under the Affordable Care Act. Every plan covers the ten essential health benefits, none can decline you or charge you more for a pre-existing condition, and most buyers qualify for financial assistance toward the premium.

Who is eligible for ACA coverage?

US citizens and lawfully present residents who do not have affordable coverage through an employer, Medicare or Medicaid. There is no age limit and no health screening. If you are turning 65 and going onto Medicare, a licensed agent can walk you through that transition instead.

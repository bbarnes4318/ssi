Availability

How plan availability works in {{state}}

Medicare Advantage availability is set by county, not by state. The plans open to you are the ones filed for your county, and that list is what a licensed agent compares with you.

1

Plans are filed by county

Each plan files the counties it will serve, so two people in {{state}} can have entirely different options depending on where they live.

2

A relative’s plan may not be yours

A plan a friend or relative has in another county may not be offered in yours. Only the list filed for your county counts.

3

Ask, with your ZIP code

Call 1-888-957-3337 (TTY 711), or use the Medicare Plan Finder at medicare.gov with your ZIP code.

Enrollment

When you can enroll in {{state}}

You can join, switch or leave a Medicare Advantage plan only during set enrollment periods. Four apply to most people.

7 months around your 65th birthday

Initial Enrollment Period

The three months before the month you turn 65, that month, and the three months after. If you are already receiving Social Security, Part A and Part B enrollment is usually automatic; a Medicare Advantage plan is a separate choice.

October 15 – December 7, every year

Annual Enrollment Period

Each fall, anyone with Medicare can join, switch or drop a Medicare Advantage plan for coverage starting January 1.

January 1 – March 31, every year

Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period

People already in a Medicare Advantage plan can make one change: switch to another Medicare Advantage plan, or return to Original Medicare.

When something in your life changes

Special Enrollment Periods

Certain life events open a window outside those dates: moving to a new county, losing other coverage, or a change in eligibility for Medicaid or Extra Help, among others. Whether an event qualifies you is worth confirming rather than assuming; a licensed agent or {{shipName}} can tell you.

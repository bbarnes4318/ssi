Medicare Advantage Plans in Texas

Texas has 254 counties, more than any other state, and Medicare Advantage availability is decided county by county, not statewide. The plans filed for Harris County are not the plans filed for Lubbock, Hidalgo or a Panhandle county, and with 254 lists in play the only reliable answer to “what is available” is the one for your own address.

A licensed agent in our Greenwood Village, Colorado office compares the Medicare Advantage plans available where you live in Texas, explains the differences, and you decide. How Medicare Advantage works is the same in every state; what is available to you is not.

How plan availability works in Texas

Medicare Advantage availability is set by county, not by state. Each plan files the counties it will serve, so two people in Texas can have entirely different options depending on where they live, and a plan a friend or relative has in another county may not be offered in yours. The plans open to you are the ones filed for your county, and that list is what a licensed agent compares with you.

Because of that, the only reliable way to find out what is available at your address is to ask. Call 1-888-957-3337 (TTY 711), or use the Medicare Plan Finder at medicare.gov with your ZIP code.

Free Medicare help in Texas

Every state runs a State Health Insurance Assistance Program, federally funded and free, staffed by trained counselors who do not sell insurance. In Texas it is Health Information, Counseling, and Advocacy Program (HICAP), reachable at 1-800-252-9240. It is a good place to get an independent second opinion on anything a licensed agent, including ours, tells you. Program name and contact details are as listed by the national SHIP directory at shiphelp.org.

When you can enroll

Initial Enrollment Period. The seven months around your 65th birthday: the three months before the month you turn 65, that month, and the three months after. If you are already receiving Social Security, Part A and Part B enrollment is usually automatic; a Medicare Advantage plan is a separate choice.

Annual Enrollment Period. Each fall, from October 15 to December 7, anyone with Medicare can join, switch or drop a Medicare Advantage plan for coverage starting January 1.

Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period. From January 1 to March 31, people already in a Medicare Advantage plan can make one change: switch to another Medicare Advantage plan, or return to Original Medicare.

Special Enrollment Periods. Certain life events open a window outside those dates: moving to a new county, losing other coverage, or a change in eligibility for Medicaid or Extra Help, among others. Whether an event qualifies you is worth confirming rather than assuming; a licensed agent or Health Information, Counseling, and Advocacy Program (HICAP) can tell you.

Texas insurance regulation

Medicare Advantage plans are approved and overseen federally by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. The agents and agencies that sell them in Texas are licensed by the Texas Department of Insurance, which also takes consumer complaints about how a plan was sold.

Common questions

Why do my options differ from a relative’s elsewhere in Texas?

Because Medicare Advantage plans file by county, and Texas has 254 of them. A plan available in Dallas County may not be filed for Tarrant, and a plan in San Antonio may not exist in the Valley. A licensed agent compares only the plans filed for your county.

Where can Texans get free, independent Medicare help?

From Texas’s State Health Insurance Assistance Program, called the Health Information, Counseling, and Advocacy Program (HICAP), run through Texas Health and Human Services, at 1-800-252-9240. Counselors are trained, free and do not sell insurance.

I live in a rural Texas county. Will I have any Medicare Advantage options?

Possibly fewer than a metro county, and in some counties very few. That is exactly why the county-level check matters; if the list is short or empty, a licensed agent can tell you what the alternatives are rather than leaving you to guess.

When are the enrollment windows?

The Initial Enrollment Period around your 65th birthday, the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period from January 1 to March 31 for people already in a plan, and Special Enrollment Periods after qualifying events such as a move.

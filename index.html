    What to expect when you call

    Same-day quotes

    The agent asks your age, state, tobacco use and the carrier’s health questions, then reads you the monthly premium from each carrier that will take your case. The call takes about ten minutes.

    No medical exams

    There is never an exam, blood test or nurse visit. Most people qualify with a few yes-or-no health questions over the phone, and guaranteed acceptance plans have none.

    A short phone application

    The application is taken over the phone: your date of birth, the health questions, the person you want paid and how you want to pay the premium. Guaranteed acceptance plans pay the full benefit after two years; the rest pay the full amount from the day you are approved.

    Final expense insurance FAQs

    Anything not covered here, a licensed agent will answer at 1-888-957-3337.

    How much coverage can I get?

    Depending on age and health, from $5,000 up to $25,000 with most carriers, and higher amounts at younger ages.

    Is the number on this page a quote?

    No. The estimator shows a monthly range based on published 2026 market averages. A licensed agent gives you an actual quote from a specific carrier after a few health questions, and that is the price you would pay.

    Can I qualify if I have a pre-existing condition?

    Yes. Common conditions such as controlled diabetes or high blood pressure usually pass the health questions; for those that do not, there is guaranteed acceptance. See which conditions affect approval.

    Do I have to give my contact information to see prices?

    No. The estimator asks only your age, gender, coverage amount and tobacco use. You share your name and phone number only if you want a licensed agent to call you.

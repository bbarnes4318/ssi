# FMO compliance review packet — /medicare/ on ssifinalexpense.com

**Site:** ssifinalexpense.com (Senior Solutions Insurance)
**Page under review:** `https://ssifinalexpense.com/medicare/`
**Prepared:** 10 September 2026
**Status of the page:** Rewrite drafted, not published. Held pending your
determination. While held, the live page is set to `noindex, follow`.

## What we are asking for

The current `/medicare/` page claims Medicare Advantage plans come at "no cost
to the beneficiary," names specific supplemental benefits, and carries no TPMO
disclaimer. We have already removed the false-cost claim from the live page.

The proposed replacement page below is written as general information about
how Medicare Advantage works and what a licensed agent can compare. It names no
plan benefit, no premium figure, no carrier-specific feature and no plan
specifics of any kind. The only call to action is a phone number. Carrier
logos (Anthem, Cigna, Humana, Devoted, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare) remain on the
page as they are today.

We need the four questions below answered before the rewrite is published.

## Questions

1. With all plan-specific benefits removed, does this page meet CMS's
   definition of marketing, or does it fall under communications?
2. If it is marketing, does it require carrier approval and annual HPMS
   filing, and which carriers do we file with first?
3. What is the exact TPMO disclaimer text we should use for the current plan
   year, including whether the SHIP reference stays in?
4. Which Medicare Advantage and Part D organizations do we need to name on
   the page, and how many organizations and products should the disclaimer
   state?

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I keep my doctor?**
It depends on the plan you choose, but many Medicare Advantage plans have
extensive networks of providers, giving you plenty of options to continue
seeing your preferred doctors.

**How do I know if I am eligible for Medicare Advantage?**
Eligibility typically requires enrollment in both Medicare Part A and Part B.
Our agents can help you determine your eligibility during a consultation.

**What happens if I want to switch plans later?**
You can usually switch Medicare Advantage plans during the Annual Enrollment
Period (AEP) or other special enrollment periods, depending on your
circumstances.

**What is Medicare Advantage?**
Medicare Advantage, also called Medicare Part C, is a way to receive your
Medicare Part A and Part B coverage through a private insurance company
approved by Medicare, instead of directly through Original Medicare. Plans
must cover everything Original Medicare covers, and each plan sets its own
costs, provider network and rules.

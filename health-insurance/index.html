Plan categories

What the four metal tiers mean

Every marketplace plan is Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum. The metal says nothing about quality of care; it is the split between what you pay in premium each month and what you pay when you use care. The percentages are averages across a typical population, set by federal rule.

Required by law

The ten essential health benefits

Under the Affordable Care Act, every marketplace plan in every tier must cover these ten categories of care. Plans differ in what you pay for them, not in whether they are covered.

  1. Ambulatory patient servicesOutpatient care you get without being admitted to a hospital
  2. Emergency servicesEmergency room care
  3. HospitalizationSurgery and overnight stays
  4. Pregnancy, maternity and newborn careBoth before and after birth
  5. Mental health and substance use disorder servicesIncluding behavioral health treatment
  6. Prescription drugsCovered medications, per the plan’s formulary
  7. Rehabilitative and habilitative services and devicesHelp gaining or recovering skills after an injury, disability or chronic condition
  8. Laboratory servicesTests and blood work
  9. Preventive and wellness servicesScreenings, vaccines and chronic disease management
  10. Pediatric servicesIncluding dental and vision care for children (adult dental and vision are not essential health benefits)

Plans must also cover birth control and breastfeeding support. Source: HealthCare.gov, what marketplace plans cover.

Questions

Health insurance FAQs

What is the ACA Open Enrollment period?

Open Enrollment is the annual window when anyone can sign up for a marketplace plan or change the one they have. It generally runs from November into January, and exact dates vary by state. Outside that window you need a qualifying life event — losing job coverage, moving, marriage, or a new child — to enroll.

Are subsidies available to lower my premium?

Often, yes. Premium tax credits are based on your household income and household size, and they are applied directly to your monthly premium rather than paid back at tax time. We check what you qualify for before you pick a plan, so you are comparing what you would actually pay.

What is ACA Health Insurance?

ACA plans are the individual and family health plans sold through the health insurance marketplace under the Affordable Care Act. Every plan covers the ten essential health benefits, none can decline you or charge you more for a pre-existing condition, and most buyers qualify for financial assistance toward the premium.

Who is eligible for ACA coverage?

US citizens and lawfully present residents who do not have affordable coverage through an employer, Medicare or Medicaid. There is no age limit and no health screening. If you are turning 65 and going onto Medicare, a licensed agent can walk you through that transition instead.

Health insurance FAQs

What is the ACA Open Enrollment period?

It is the time each year when you can sign up for ACA health insurance plans, typically running from November to January.

Are subsidies available to lower my premium?

Yes, subsidies are available for eligible individuals based on income and family size.

What is ACA Health Insurance?

ACA health insurance, also known as Obamacare, is a program designed to make health coverage more affordable and accessible.

Who is eligible for ACA coverage?

U.S. citizens or legal residents without affordable employer coverage may qualify.
